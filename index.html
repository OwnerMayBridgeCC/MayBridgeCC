Why the bridge matters

We exist to close the gap.

Between “someone I love needs help” and “I know what to do next,” there should be a bridge.

That bridge is MayBridge. Our brand is built around connection: families on one side, capable providers on the other, and a clearer path between them. We are not building just another directory. We are building a trusted care marketplace and family care command center designed to make fragmented support easier to navigate.

Our promise: practical options, clearer decisions and a more connected path forward.

Find the right kind of support

One bridge. Many care needs.

MayBridge is designed to help families start in one place, even when they are not yet sure what kind of support they need.

01

Companionship & everyday support

Connections for companionship, errands, meal preparation, routines and help staying engaged and independent.

02

Transportation & practical help

Support for appointments, essential errands, household needs and other day-to-day gaps that can overwhelm families.

03

Care coordination

Organize options, compare next steps and get help navigating practical care arrangements without starting over every time.

04

Respite & backup care

Explore support when a regular caregiver is unavailable, family members need relief or a care plan changes.

05

Personal care

Request introductions to appropriate organizations for hands-on support based on location, needs and availability.

06

Licensed nursing connections

For clinical or nursing-level needs, MayBridge connects users with appropriately licensed providers or organizations that assess and manage care.

MayBridge is a marketplace and coordination platform. Provider availability, scope and suitability must be confirmed. MayBridge does not represent this website as an emergency service or as a substitute for a provider’s clinical assessment. For emergencies, call 911.

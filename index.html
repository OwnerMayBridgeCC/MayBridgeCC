What kind of support do you need?

Our initial focus is connecting families with established provider organizations. Availability and suitability are confirmed individually before an introduction.

Companion & everyday support

Explore companionship, errands, meal preparation and help with everyday routines. Tell us your preferred location and schedule.

Concierge & coordination

Ask about paid help organizing support options, appointments and practical arrangements. We confirm the scope and quote before work begins.

Personal & nursing care

Request an introduction to an appropriately licensed organization for personal care or nursing needs. The provider assesses needs and manages clinical care.

We are accepting inquiries and provider applications as the network develops. A request does not confirm available care or book a visit. MayBridge does not currently offer direct nursing services through this website. For an emergency, call 911.

Grow your local connections

Apply to join MayBridge's developing provider network. We are prioritizing established organizations with appropriate licensing and insurance. Individuals, including nurses, may register interest for a future pathway.

Application is free and does not create a subscription. Any future paid company listing or network access offer will include its price and terms before you opt in. Applying does not guarantee approval, client introductions, employment or earnings.

Privacy notice

Effective September 14, 2026. MayBridge Care Collective LLC uses the contact, service preference and professional information you voluntarily submit to respond to inquiries, review applications and discuss potential introductions. Forms are delivered through FormSubmit to our business email; those services process submissions under their own policies. Hosting providers may process routine request and security logs.

Please provide only your own contact details or information you are authorized to share. Do not submit medical records, diagnoses, Social Security numbers, dates of birth, financial details or information about children. This is an initial inquiry form, not a clinical intake system.

We request your permission before sharing your contact details for a provider introduction. Your form information is not posted publicly. Separate screening vendors collect any sensitive screening information on their own sites. Contact support@maybridgecarecollective.com to request access, correction or deletion of your inquiry, subject to any applicable retention obligations. Third-party retention and processing may differ.

Website & inquiry terms

This website accepts non-emergency inquiries and provider applications. Submitting a form does not create a care agreement, employment relationship, confirmed placement or purchase. Information is reviewed before any introduction or service offer.

Providers are responsible for their own services, assessments, staffing, professional scope, licensing and care agreements. MayBridge does not provide medical advice or confirm insurance, Medicare, Medicaid, TRICARE or VA coverage. Confirm service scope, costs and coverage directly with the proposed provider.

Any paid MayBridge coordination service or company network plan requires a separate written offer stating scope, price, cancellation and refund terms before payment. Application submission itself is free. No client volume, income or care availability is guaranteed.

Verification information must be reviewed before any approval is represented. External links do not imply that a check has been ordered, completed or approved. Questions: support@maybridgecarecollective.com.

Our mission

Bridge the distance between needing care and finding trusted support.

Care decisions deserve clarity.

Families often begin a care search during moments of change, pressure, or uncertainty. MayBridge exists to replace scattered searching with a clear path to qualified local options and an organized place for the details that matter.

What we are building

A member-supported care marketplace where approved provider profiles can be discovered by location and need, while families maintain continuity through care-recipient profiles, requests, bookings, and service history.

Our promise

Make the next step clearer

Give families useful information without overwhelming them.

Make trust visible

Show approval, credential, and screening status plainly.

Keep people at the center

Use technology to strengthen human care—not replace it.
